# Home-side bet rules (30 Sep 2026)

**Nothing passes; the live rule (4+ either side) stays.** A rule needs more units than the live rule on every window and a placebo p at or under 0.1.

Home side = the model's side is the home team at a non-neutral site. The lean-adjusted rules take the model's home lean off every edge before the 4+ cut (the walk-forward one uses the mean lean of every earlier season).
