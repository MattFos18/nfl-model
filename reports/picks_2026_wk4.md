# Week 4, 2026: model picks

Our line is home spread / total. Edge = model minus Vegas (spread: positive favours the home side; total: positive favours the over). Win, cover and total are the model's chances for each side at the current line (the win and total chances calibrated on the backtest, picks.home_calibration and picks.over_calibration, 27 Sep 2026; the raw p_home stays in the csv, and the totals flag reads the raw p_over_emp there); 52.4% is break-even at -110.
Bets: the spread when the edge is 4+ points (on the current model that cut is 79-51 on the tuning window and 37-18 held out (weeks 1 to 17), and 69-56 on the untouched 2015 to 2018 window); the under at a 55%+ raw chance (the totals flag); the under in outdoor games with forecast wind 10+ mph (the wind under). Overs are never bet. No bets in Week 18, where resting starters make the line smarter than the ratings. Stake: one unit a bet. The other shadow columns are rules logged and graded but never bet (4.5+ edge; 4+ edge, model's side the underdog or pick'em; 4+ edge, weeks 1 to 13 only; boosted trees alone, 5+ edge; Under, 59%+ chance in weeks 1 to 3, 55%+ after; 4+ edge on +2.5, +3, -3 or -3.5, half a point bought on or off 3 at -125; 4+ edge on road sides, 6+ on home sides; 4+ edge, road sides only; Under, 60%+ chance; Under, 55%+ chance, prime time (TNF, SNF, MNF) only; 6-point teaser leg on dogs at +1.5 to +2.5, any game (a leg of a two-team teaser at -130); Under, forecast rain chance 50%+ (outdoor games); Under, forecast temperature below 32 F (outdoor games); Under, model total 3+ points below the line; Under, 55%+ chance and forecast wind 10+ mph; Total, boosted trees' own total 9.5%+ of the line off it, either side; West Coast or Mountain team on the road at 1pm ET, any game; 4+ edge, model's side the road underdog; 4+ edge, model's side a dog at +0.5 to +3; 3.5+ edge on dogs, 4+ on every other side; 4+ edge, weeks 1 to 4 only; 4+ edge, weeks 1 to 15 only; 6+ edge), to decide the rule on live games.
